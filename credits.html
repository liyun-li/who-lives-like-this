Credits & sources

Every drawing is a measured drawing from the Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS), National Park Service — a U.S. government work in the public domain. Files came via Wikimedia Commons or the Library of Congress Prints & Photographs Division. For the game each sheet was cropped, recolored blueprint-blue, and had title blocks, owner/house names, places, survey numbers, dates and draftsman credits whited out. Room labels were kept and re-lettered in a larger type for phones (wording as on the sheet, except name-bearing labels shortened to a generic word); plans were cropped to the building and wide plans turned 90°.

Facts on the reveal come from the HABS significance notes on the linked source page, plus the extra fact sources listed.
