Credits & sources

Almost every drawing is a measured drawing from the Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS) or its sister programs HAER (Historic American Engineering Record) and HALS (Historic American Landscapes Survey), National Park Service — U.S. government works in the public domain. Two rounds use 1870s–90s newspaper diagrams, public domain by age. Files came via Wikimedia Commons or the Library of Congress Prints & Photographs Division. For the game each sheet was cropped, recolored blueprint-blue, and had title blocks, owner/house names, places, survey numbers, dates and draftsman credits whited out. The sheets' original lettering was erased; room labels now appear as tap-to-show tags on dots placed in each room (wording as on the sheet, except name-bearing labels shortened to a generic word); plans were cropped to the building and wide plans turned 90°.

Facts on the reveal come from the HABS significance notes on the linked source page, plus the extra fact sources listed.
